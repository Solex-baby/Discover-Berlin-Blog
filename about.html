About Me — My Journey in Berlin

I came to Berlin almost by accident. I planned to stay only two weeks, just long enough to satisfy a quiet curiosity about a city that carries its past so openly.

ut the more I walked its streets, the more I felt a quiet pull—some places feel like they choose you before you choose them. Berlin became that place for me, asking me to slow down and notice the stories etched into its walls.

Learning the City by Getting Lost

My exploration started the way most meaningful adventures do—by getting lost. I followed unfamiliar streets, wandered into old courtyards, and let the Spree River lead me into neighborhoods far from any guidebook.

Along the way, I discovered that curiosity works better than any map, especially in a city that rewards those who take the long, quiet route. The small finds—hidden murals, forgotten corners, a café tucked behind a garden—kept me walking.

Collecting Moments Worth Sharing

I began writing simply to remember the moments that made me feel at home here: the echo beneath the cathedral dome, the warmth of a market on cold mornings, the softness of Berlin at sunset.

Over time, these notes grew into stories for travelers seeking more than sightseeing. Berlin taught me that every corner holds a fragment of a story, and if you move slowly enough, the city will share it with you.
